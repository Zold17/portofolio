Data Labeling & Sentiment Classifier

A web app that simulates annotator work. You label Indonesian product reviews by hand, then compare your labels with an automatic classifier and see where they disagree.

What it does

  • Manual labeling as positive negative neutral
  • Automatic prediction from an Indonesian word lexicon, written from scratch with no NLP libraries
  • Accuracy against manual labels and a list of mismatched items
  • CSV export of the labeled results

How the negation rule works

tidak bagus → bagus +1 “tidak” flips it → negative

An illustration of the rule, not a row from the dataset.

Result

Agreement
about 80% with manual labels
Sample
30 dummy reviews (wooden products)
Stack
Python, Pandas, Streamlit

Known limits

  • Misses negation when the negation word is more than one word away
  • No prediction when a sentence has no dictionary words

Next

Wider negation handling, a bigger lexicon, Naive Bayes or transformer models trained on labeled data, and uploading your own dataset.
